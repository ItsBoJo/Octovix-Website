Coming soon to the App Store

Track the nutrients other apps ignore.

BlueLog counts calories and macros, then keeps going: vitamins, minerals, fiber, water and weight trends.

Your whole day on one screen.

Every box is a number you chose to watch. Tap one to open its history, or edit the grid until the screen matches how you actually eat.

Built for the numbers most trackers drop.

Calories are the easy part. BlueLog keeps the rest of the label in view, so a day that hits its macros does not quietly miss everything else.

Micronutrients, measured against real goals

Vitamin C, vitamin D, calcium, fiber and sodium each sit on the home grid with the amount logged and how far it is from the day's target.

A grid you arrange

Add a box, drop one you never read, and put the rest in the order you want to see them.

Water and calories at a glance

The day's calorie progress sits in the header and water is a single tap to add.

Weight as a trend you can actually read

Seven days, thirty days or a year, plotted against your goal line rather than a single morning's reading.

Three taps, then get on with your day.

Logging should take seconds. What you do with the numbers afterwards is where the app earns its place.

Log what you ate

Add a meal and the day's totals move with it, calories and micronutrients together.

See where the gaps are

Each box shows how close the day is to its goal, so a shortfall is obvious before bedtime.

Adjust with the trend

Weight and nutrient history build up over weeks and show which changes actually held.
